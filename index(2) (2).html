How it works

Tap. Pay. Done.

The NFC card identifies the member wallet. The wallet balance is managed by the café system.

Get your member card

2

Add café credits

Make payment to the café and request/receive wallet credits.

3

Tap at checkout

The staff creates your bill and your NFC card is used to identify your wallet.

4

Track everything

Your member account keeps your balance and transaction history available.
